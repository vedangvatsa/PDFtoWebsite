# No-Slop Publishing Rules

Public copy of the mandatory style rules used on CVin.Bio.

See the full file in the repository root: `noslop.md`.

## Quick rules

- No em dashes (`—`) or en dashes (`–`). Use periods or hyphens.
- No curly quotes. Use straight quotes.
- No ellipsis character (`…`). Use `...` or finish the sentence.
- No empty AI filler ("delve", "landscape", "unlock your potential").
- Short sentences. Facts first.

All job descriptions on this site are cleaned for punctuation before display.
