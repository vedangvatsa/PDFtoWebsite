Problem & Market

The AI agent economy needs hiring infrastructure. None exists.

By 2027, 40% of enterprise apps will embed AI agents (Gartner). AI engineer postings grew 143% YoY. Agent orchestration, RAG pipelines, and autonomous workflows are the fastest-growing job categories.

But hiring still runs on keyword matching and recruiter fees. A single senior hire costs $50K+ in fees and takes 89 days. No platform structures talent data so that agents on either side, whether for job seekers or employers, can query it programmatically.

AI sourcing startups raised $900M+ but all automate the recruiter. None build the structured data layer agents need.
